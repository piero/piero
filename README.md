### Hi, I'm Piero 🖖

**Staff/Lead Software Engineer** building backend platforms, real-time data pipelines and multi-agent AI systems.

I've been building software for 20+ years, at startups, scale-ups and at Amazon, and I like taking a vague problem all the way to production.

I'm curious by nature, and that's shaped my career: I've moved from real-time streaming and networking to video platforms, then NLP at scale, IoT and machine learning, and now LLM agents. Each time I learned a new field from scratch and shipped production systems in it. Learning new things is the part of the job I enjoy most.

- **Now:** Lead Software Engineer at Nuraxi.ai, building LLM pipelines that turn clinical notes into structured records

- **Before:** real-time IoT decision pipelines for UK social care (Lilli), 4M+ docs/day NLP pipelines (Signal AI), Prime Video on PlayStation 3 and Fire TV (Amazon)

- **Fun fact:** DTNPerf, the tool from my university thesis, is used at NASA JPL and on the ISS 🌍 ))) · · · · · · ((( 🛰️

- Currently based in Italy, working remotely since 2021
